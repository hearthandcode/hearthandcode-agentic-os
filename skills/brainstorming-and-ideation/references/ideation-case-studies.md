# Ideation Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Solo Ideation for a Newsletter Name

**Session type:** Solo, 30 minutes
**Problem:** A local food newsletter needs a new name and branding angle. Current name "Weekend Eats" feels generic and does not differentiate.
**Methods used:** Forced analogy (hospitality/restaurants as a city), constraint flips, dot-voting

### Session timeline

**0:00-0:02** — Write the prompt: "Name a weekly newsletter for people who want to discover new neighborhood restaurants, not just the popular ones. The name should suggest exploration, local knowledge, and curated discovery."

**0:02-0:10** — Technique: Forced Analogy with "Hospitality" (Hotel, restaurant, hospitality systems)
- The check-in: "The name should be 'Check-in Service'" — that means something else and is not differentiated.
- The concierge as guide → "Concierge Picks"
- The key → "Unlock Your Neighborhood"
- Room number idea → "Cell 7" (wrong, too specific)
- The hidden restaurant the locals know → "Speakeasy"
- Turn-down service thought → "The weekly discovery is delivered, not known" → "The Discovery"

New ideas generated:

1. The local note — 2. Between the lines — 3. Your block — 4. The Salt (ambient) — 5. The Hidden Spoon — 6. The Guide — 7. Front Door — 8. The Insider — 9. The Counter — 10. Walk By — 11. The Table — 12. The Dish — 13. The Prep

**0:10-0:20** — Technique: Constraint Matrix

Apply three constraints: The name must (a) be no more than 3 words, (b) feel like a single place, (c) feel aspirational, not descriptive.

- 6 word pairs with the idea of "hunting (hunt) and discovery":

direction + eating: [Found Feast, Discovery Plate]
city + friends: [The Local, The Messenger]
hidden + taste: [The Hidden Tasting, Discovery Plate]
direction + search: [Compass Taste, The Divider"
route + room: [The Table, The Side, The Spread, The Break]

Generated: 14. Roam  — 15. The Stroll — 16. The Path — 17. A Dish — 18. Found — 19. The Street — 20. A Seat — 21. The Table — 22. The Inside Scoop — 23. The Lane — 24. The Dish — 25. The Fork — 26. A Walk — 27. The Corner — 28. The Passage — 29. The Season

**00:20-00:25** — Clustering and dot-voting

Clusters:
- Action: (Walk, Roam, Path, Itinerary, A Walk, The Lantern)
- place: (Front Door, The Counter, The Table, The Table, The Lane, The Corner)
- Score: (The Insiders, The Inside Scoop, The Guide)
- Food: (A Dish, The Fork, The Saloon, The Hidden Spoon)
- Generic: (The Local Note, "The... line...), etc.
Second name specific: being location traverses.

Dot-vote:
Action cluster wins: Roam (3), Walk/Way (2) and Discovery: The Counter (3).
Food cluster: The Fork (2).

**00:25-00:30** — Evaluation:

Winner: "The Counter." Because:
- "App" already used but in a difference.
Shortlist: The Counter, Roam, The Table.

**Outcome:** The client chose "The Counter" — connects to the diner bar, chef's counter, people counter. It made the VC pitch feel fresh."

## Case Study 2: Team Ideation for Community Event

**Session setup:** 6 people, 60 minutes
**Problem:** "We have a local creator community that meets monthly. Attendance is dropping from 20 to 8. How can we redesign the event to bring people back?"

**Methods used:** Brainwriting (6-3-5), SCAMPER, dot-voting (weighted)

**02 minutes: warm up:**
Blank warm-up: 2 minutes each write "one thing you would change about the event."

**02-17 (15 min) — Brainwriting (6-3-5 method)**
- 6 people, each 3 ideas on a sheet, 5 minutes.
- Pass the sheet to the write 3 new ones.
6 rounds = 108 raw ideas.

**Raw output sample:**
- Alternate host from the community
- A monthly topic
- Bring sticker (badge-style to keep conversation notes)
- The "best 15" corner in the event
- The event time is not the same day of the week
- Host in different neighborhood each month
- Invite a local business to present
- Breakout by skill level
- 5-minute "networking" before the event
- Recording for those who come late
- Ask long-time members to lead a section
- Venue: a bookstore, rather than the usual room
- Pets allowed on weekend
- Bring open-source project to the event for community input
- Plus 18 more)

** —17-32 (15 min) — Sweat again: "Now figure out how to do it in the first few minutes of the event"

matching:
- Bingo game: "first attendance that wins, it"s a game of 5, conversation"
- "Bring a question about something you have not yet tried" — the only activity
- "In the new people, they do a 2-minute intro + a question"
- "The ratio: the first 10 minutes is a game, like" — this: when you arrive you flip the conversation slot"
- "Pre-event topic: we get dedicated to break the box before the meeting"
- "In town home: each volunteer teaches you how to do something
- "People draw a map of something they are working on and show it at the table"
- "Food: bring potluck that matches the theme of the month"
- "Coffee: the mind mapping on a big whiteboard as people enter"
- "The conversation: the hour is unlock a new activity% increase (1-2) of prior events"
- "Theme-pert: simple trivia about people who"

### — 32-47 (15 min) — SCAMPER on the worst idea + a better one:

For the worsts: "Don't hold an event at all, just run a shop automation."

Improvement: instead, publish each month a pack: event box: prompt cards, stickers, a simple activity.

Idea: "Seat-charge Box." Ship a "meeting package" to registered attendees.

**— 47-60 — Convergence:**

1. Clustering:
- Location switch: different neighborhoods, bookstores, parks
- The formatting: bingo, discussion password, project showcase
- The format: time blocks, food, pets
- The content: featured guest, community project support, lecture

2. Weighting scores:
- "Monthly packs" (feasibility 4, novelty 4, fit 5) =  4*3+5*4+4*5 = 12+20+20 = 52
- "Breakout by skill level" (f4, n3, f5) = 4*4+3*5+5*5 = 16+15+25 = 56
- "Invite a local guest" (f4, n3, f3) = 4*4+3*5+3*5 = 16+15+15 = 46
- "Pets allowed, weekend" (f 4, n 4, f 2) = 4*4+4*5+2*5 = 16+20+10 = 46

**Win:** "Breakout by skill level" (56) — niche to implement: each month choose either beginner-friendly or advanced-format.

**Edits:** "We really need to vary the venue as well. rotate."

**Final shortlist:**
1. Breakout by skill level (decided)
2. Civic venue rotation once per quarter
3. Pre-event: community attendees complete a card.

**Decision Record:**
- The next 3 events will run the skill-level breakout.
- Venue rotation is set for month 2.
- Community project 'show and tell' is added to month 1 only.

**Results after 3 cycles:** Attendance went from average 15, to 40 after 4 meetups.
