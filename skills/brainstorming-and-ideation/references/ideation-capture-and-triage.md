# Ideation Capture and Triage — Checklist

## Capture Formats

**Physical:**
- Large sticky notes: one idea per note. Post on a giant wall.
- Index cards: same principle, portable. Sortable in a shoebox.
- Whiteboard: only for the initial divergence write. Do not leave ideas on a whiteboard — they will be lost after the session.

**Digital:**
- Shared spreadsheet (for which each idea is a row).
- Collaborative board or document that all participants can see.
- A fixed recording: record the session and extract ideas later — not ideal because judgment is slow and the recording may not be there.

**The critical rule:** Always capture in a way that ideas at any moment can be moved, sorted, or eliminated without rewriting. Each idea is a discrete editable unit.

### Captured Format

Each idea capture should have, at minimum:

- ID number (sequential)
- Idea (in one short paragraph)
- Source (which technique produced it)
- Divergence round (anonymous — optional)

### Capturing Metadata (Optional)

During divergence, you can optionally collect one metadata field to evaluate later:
- "Confidence": It is not a critique. It is measured by: "this is really exciting to me."

But collect share at most. Secondary metadata is done after the fact.

## Binary Evaluation on the Fly (Parking Lot)

During divergence, concerns will arise. The parking lot is the structured way to capture those concerns without deviating the flow.

**Parking Lot Rule:**
- If someone has a concern about an idea, write it on a sticky note.
- The note goes on the parking lot sheet. The person can continue generating ideas.
- The only exception: a concern that would kill the session immediately (legal safety risk) — in which case you pause the session briefly, resolve the hazard, and restart.

The parking lot is reviewed during convergence — not earlier.

## Triage Process

After convergence, triage the resulting 3-5 shortlisted ideas:

### Level 1: Archive

The idea is moved to the archive with a note:
```
ID: #
Idea: [one line]
Why not selected: [brief]
Resurface condition: [if any]
```

### Level 2: Hold
The idea stays in the shortlist but require external conditions to proceed:
- "Yes, but only if X happens."
- "Yes, but needs further research on Y."

### Level 3: Purge to execution
The idea flagged for next steps: design the prototype, estimate, test, project plan.

## Decision Record Format

After the session, record the decisions reached:

```
Session: [Name of session]
Date:
Prompt: [...]

Decision 1:
- Shortlist Model: [list of decisions]
- What to achieve by EOD: [what does it mean?]
- What conditions would it be pivot/stop? [go/cancel condition]
- Responsible: [who def?]

Decision 2:
...
```

The decision record is a reusable artifact. Capture enough fidelity to reconstruct the session.

## Capture Anti-Patterns

- **Too long to capture:** If an idea takes more than 10 seconds to write, it is not ready. Select the version of the idea that requires one line.
- **Digital capture lags:** typing live ideas slows down the whole session. Use a scribe who does not generate ideas.
- **Recording grief:** If a participant is late: Let the session, not later.
- **Cleaning ideas during capture:** "Let me consolidate these two." No. Distillation is an act of judgment during the divergence.
- **Loss of continuity:** The capture tool disappears or the document is closed before the session ends.
