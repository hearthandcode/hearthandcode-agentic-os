# Worked Ideation Session: "Urban Field Guide" Podcast

## Session Overview

**Session type:** Solo
**Duration:** 45 minutes (30 minutes divergence + 10 minutes convergence + 5 minutes decisions)
**Prompt:** "How might we design a podcast concept called 'Urban Field Guide' — a show that helps people notice and understand the nature in their city, from birds on the sidewalk to plants pushing through concrete — that is engaging enough to recommend and practical enough to change how listeners walk through their city?"

**Success criteria:** 30+ raw ideas → clustered → shortlist of 3 with scored evaluation.

---

## Phase 1: Divergence (00:00 – 00:30)

### Round 1 (00:00–00:15): SCAMPER on the baseline concept

Baseline: "A narrator describes urban wildlife you might encounter on your walk."

1. Substitute: Replace the narrator with ambient sound + field recordings only. Let the listener identify what they hear.
2. Substitute: Replace generic voice with a rotating set of local residents, each describing the wildlife on their block.
3. Combine: Merge the field guide with a geolocation app — episodes change content based on where the listener is standing.
4. Combine: Pair each episode with a short nature-craft activity: build a moth net from a takeout lid.
5. Adapt: Borrow from nature-deficit-disorder research: every episode starts with a 30-second "look up" prompt.
6. Adapt: Borrow from birding "pish" techniques — teach one audio skill per episode (identify bird by song, identify tree by bark).
7. Modify: Each episode is exactly 3 minutes, one species or artifact of the city.
8. Modify: Instead of weekly, release a "season" of 7 episodes over 7 consecutive days — a daily commute companion.
9. Put to another use: Use the field guide recordings as a soundscape for a local history walking tour.
10. Put to another use: Publish maps and checklists as printable sheets that the listener carries on a walk.

### Round 2 (00:15–offset 00:30): Worst Idea First

*Terrible ideas, intentionally terrible:*

11. "Each episode: walking to the listener to walk and clap as a form of greeting wildlife."
12. "The podcast requires a subscription to feeder cameras so you can watch squirrels in real time."
13. "Each guest is a single animal — they perform a monologue about their viewpoint on the city."
14. "Field guide in poetry — only haiku format."
15. "Episodes are generated entirely by AI analyzing waste bins and incinerating infrastructure."
16. "A 30-minute sound of rustling leaves."
17. "Every episode is a guided GPS scavenger hunt for stickers placed next to drying bird tracks."
18. "Only available to the person who reports the time of a bird passing the microphone bearing a snack."

**Flip of the worst** (take the worst and invert to get a good idea):

19. (From #12) Accessible use: a "suggested Soap Bet: Watch something vertical for five minutes — write down what you see." Instead of show, listener action.
20. (From #14) Not haiku, but a very short form: 30-second "pocket guide" for a single species of the day.
21. (From directories) Since not inviting an animal to guest-host: invite a local naturalist, a gardener, a plumber, a bird watcher — whoever knows the city from that lens. The "Guest City" host series.

### Round 3 (00:30–00:45): Constraint Flip

Constraints flipped:
- "Linear storytelling" → "The podcast has no host, it's a layered soundscape with listener choices (choose your path episodes)."
- "Formal structure" → "Each episode has no structure, just a central thought that the listener follows."
- "One full episode" → "Episodes are released in 'city layers' series — each layer focuses on a single organism for 5 episodes."
- "Rotes produced by a host or showrunner" → "The field guide is produced, not the creator: community-based, listener-forward field notes. Listeners send in their findings."

From these flips:

22. "Episodes are city layers: Layer 1 = "What's green?" Layer 2 = "What's loud?" Layer 3 = "What's on the pavement?" Each is one micro episode per season.
23. "Unstructure the trope of opening intro + segment + outro. Start the episode mid-scene."
24. Split hosting: "Every episode has two guests: one naturalist, one newcomer to the city."
25. "The 7-day series: Each day, one action for the listener to perform, then the next day the episode starts with archive of their response."
26. "Producer workflow not shown to the listener as a preview of the whole month."
27. "City partnerships: The field guide is not just podcast but also a monthly city wildlife report that folds into the podcast episode."

### Bonus (final 2 minutes): brainstorming a few more to get past 30

28. "Episode structure: Soundscape → observation → explanation → what to look for tomorrow."
29. "Co-brand with library: each episode corresponds to an item in the library collection (bird book, plant encyclopedia)."
30. "The field guide as a live streams — one microphone per neighborhood, each month audio collage of the neighborhood."
31. "Interview: street sweepers, librarians, construction workers — they have the city's eye view."
32. "No episodes: the field guide is a live 'book of city' — a listener never catches same episode twice because they're on a different path."

→ Total: 32 ideas.

## Phase 5: Final Shortlist (00:00–00:00)

| Rank | Idea | Rationale |
|------|------|-----------|
| 1 | **Daily 3-minute micro episodes (7-day series)** — Idea 7 | Highest feasibility + novelty: extremely accessible, can be produced fast, compels daily listen. Can contain soundscape, explanation, and cliffhang to next day's observation. |
| 2 | **Guest series: listener experts** — Idea 21 | Alternating perspective every season brings new audiences. Each naturalist, street worker, newcomer brings fresh perspective. Repeatability. |
| 3 | **30-second "pocket field guide" species of the day** — Idea 8 | A distributable format — any single species widely shared, perfect for social media forwarding, and works as the core content core of daily episodes. |

**Holding lot (deferred but not discarded):** Geotagged episodes (if: platform partnership arises).
**Secret:** Listener field notes (idea 19, 27) — content to be included as a supplementary component to the flagship shortlist.

## Notes

- The 30-second pocket guide and daily 3-minute episodes are actually a same idea in different lengths. Bundle as a mini-series with a micro capsule per day or per episode.
- The city worker interview is actually a strong podcast concept for variable seasons — but it is a different format and could be a separate series track.
- The session reached 32 ideas, just above the goal of 30. The constrained form of the latter (constraint flips) produced the most breakthrough-heavy suggestions — with the prompt "unstructured" generating the most novel content.
