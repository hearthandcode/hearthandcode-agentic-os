## The ten-question test

1. What does this character want, in scene one, that they will not get?
2. What do they believe that the story will break?
3. At what moment could they believe the story is over, and why would they act on that misreading?
4. Whose opinion do they weigh too heavily?
5. Name two ways the character contradicts themselves.
6. What skill do they think is their strongest that is actually a crutch?
7. What do others say about them that is both true and unfair?
8. What line would the character not cross, and how does the story bring them to it?
9. What sentence would break the character?
10. If the character does not arc, what fixed quality do they prove true?

## Revision notes (complete after revision pass)

- Are the character's actions in conflict with their stated desire in at least one scene?
- Does the character have a scene where they act against their own stated nature?
- Can the character be removed without breaking the story? (If yes, they need more integration.)
- Does the character's dialogue sound uniquely theirs when tags are removed?
