## Revision targets

After first draft, return here:

- [ ] All beats present with correct emotional movement
- [ ] No scene without a character who wants something
- [ ] Motif appears at least three times, trajectory confirmed
- [ ] Word budget balanced against structure (beat-to-length matches expected proportions)
- [ ] Thematic argument testable from the outline
