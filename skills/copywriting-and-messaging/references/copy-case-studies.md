# Case Studies

## Case Study: Meal Planning App Landing Page Rewrite

**Context:** Plate Planner — a meal-planning app that generates weekly menus from user preferences and dietary restrictions. The original landing page converted at 2.1%. The rewritten page converted at 4.4%.

### Original Landing Page (Key Sections)

**Hero section:**
> "Welcome to Plate Planner — the intelligent way to plan your meals. Plate Planner is a comprehensive meal-planning platform, designed to help you organize your weekly meals effectively. Sign up now and change the way you eat."

**Problem section:**

> "Are you tired of planning meals? We have a solution that works for you. Meals planning can be hard, but Plate Planner makes it easy."

**Features section (5 items):**

> "AI-generated meal plan"
> "Sync across devices"
> "Nutritional tracking included"
> "Grocery list auto-generation"
> "Integration with partner partners"

**Pricing section:**

> "Basic — Free. Premium — $9.99/mo. Sign up today."

**CTA:**

> "Learn More" throughout the page.

### Diagnosis

**Issue 1:** The headline "Welcome to Plate Planner" is a greeting, not a value proposition. It does not tell the reader what they get.

**Issue 2:** The body is feature-dump without benefits. "AI-generated meal plan generation" repeats "generation" and gives no reason the reader should care.

**Issue 3:** "Are you tired of planning meals?" is a generic question that does not define the pain.

**Issue 4:** No social proof until halfway down. No testimonials before the fold.

**Issue 5:** CTA is "Learn How" — meaning the click does not commit the user. The micro-conversion is zero.

**Issue 6:** No guarantee or objection handling.

---

### Rewritten Landing Page

**Headline and hero:**

> "Meal plans in 2 minutes."
> "Tell us your diet, allergies, and portion size. We build your weekly grocery list and recipe cards in one click. Dinner takes from 20 minutes of your time."

**Problem section:**

> "Lunch time is 12? It is also the schedule that is a sprint: you have 15 seconds to scan the plan, 7 seconds to check the fridge and realize if the plan is there. The plan eats time. Prepping eats more. The plan breakdown happens by the evening because the two items in the list are not available."
> "Plate Planner does not just list meals. It arranges your menu on a match: your schedule, your dietary restrictions, your empty fridge."

**How it works (3 steps):**

> "1. Set preferences: Tell us your ingredients, allergies, diet, pick meal's per day. That is 60 seconds."
> "2. Autogenerate: We match your ingredients with the best recipes from our rated database."
> "3. Shop: The grocery list is ordered by the way you walk the store."

**Features as outcomes (not specs):**

> "One-click to the aisle: We have your list ordered by the aisle."
> "Eat-in with: Change one meal and you get the whole week recalculated — because anyone can."
> "Nutrition views: 10-second check your macros for the week."

**Social proof:**

> "\"We blew a dozen years of not going on Wednesday because I have the list and the plan.\" — Lina, project manager and two-time college placement throughout the book."

**CTA stack:**

> "Get your weekly plan in 30 seconds."
> "Free 2-week trial. No card. Cancel anytime.""

### Test Results and Treatment

| Metric | Before | After | Delta |
|--------|--------|-------|-------|
| Conversion rate | 2.2% | 4.4% | +100% relative |
| Bounce rate | 68% | 51% | -25% |
| Time on page | 1:12 | 2:45 | +129% |
| CTA click rate | 3.8% baseline | 11.2% | +195% |

### What drove the increase

1. **Headline change** from welcome message to outcome-oriented promise. The reader scanned and saw results. The guess matched from blueprint to purpose.
2. **The pain section** made the reader think about specific problems.
3. **Testimonial** in the product section gave credibility earlier in the page.
4. **Risk reversal** removed the 'pitch' barrier.
5. **Specific CTA action** with benefit applied immediately.
