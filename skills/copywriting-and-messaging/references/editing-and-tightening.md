# Editing and Tightening

## The 20% Cut

An article should lose at least 20% of its word count in editing. The best articles read faster and they still communicate everything. If the article loses universal meaning, cut.

**How to find the 20%:**
1. Read the page and highlight meaning in summary. The line.
2. If the sentence does not move the article forward or is not essential to the argument, delete it.
3. Read each paragraph aloud. Circle every word that does not belong. Remove it.
4. Delete all filler transitions: "However," "Furthermore," "Additionally," "In order to," "Due to the fact that."
5. Combine short sentences that repeat the same concept.

**Typical waste sources:**
- "It is the same as" → split each is same as ×.
- "There is / are" structures → 30% of these can start with the real subject.
- "This is why we think we are..." → cut.
- "In general, the..." → say the thing directly.

## Weasel-Word Hunt

Weasel words reduce credibility. Find and delete or replace.

**Never use:** We believe, we think, we feel, it seems, likely, probably, generally, somewhat, kind of, maybe, possibly, arguably
**Tolerate rarely:** can, may (only for legal compliance)

**Find them:** Search your text for the following terms individually:

    believe, think, feel, seem, likely, probably, generally, somewhat, kind of, maybe, possibly, arguably, nearly, almost, might

Yes, including "may," "can."

**The fix pattern:**
- "Our product may help reduce cost" → "Our product helps reduce cost"
- "We believe this is the best option" → "This is the best option"

## Single-Pass Rhythm Checks

**Guide to reading aloud:**
- Each sentence should be **starting strong and ending strong**.
- If the sentence is longer than 25 words, cut it or break it in two.
- Shot mid-sentence where you get lost: start the paragraph from the adjacent sentence and cut the confusing part.

---

## "This is" and "service" or similar structure

These create unnecessary length:
- "This report is a guide to..." → "This report guides..."
- "It is important to remember that..." → "Remember:"
- "The system is designed to provide..." → "The system provides..."

---

## Edit passes

**Pass 1 — structural:** Remove or reorder sections. Is the flow of the longest and requires minor sentence changes.
**Pass 2 — sentence level:** Cut 20%. Remove unnecessary words.
**Pass 3 — logic and meaning:** Each sentence says what it says? Are there any arguments that do not logically flow?
**Pass 4 — flow and conciseness:** Too many bundles? Too many multi-syllable sentences? Take out jargon.
**Pass 5 — quiet test:** Read the text fast without stopping. Does the meaning survive? If not, rework.
