## Short Analysis

| Weakness | Principle Violated | Fix |
|----------|-------------------|-----|
| Hero does not describe why | Clarity over cleverness (inverted: greeting is not a value proposition) | Lead with the outcome: time saved, not words |
| "AI-generated" is technical | Benefits over features | "Week menu in one click" |
| No pain specificity | One reader | Target the busy 5p.m. scrambler |
| Risk avoidance | Conversion copy — no tip reversal | Guarantee statement near CTA |
| CTA not actionable | Conversion (start with verb, not benefit start) | "Plan your first week" — no account display] |

## Test Plan (Priorities)

1. **Headline test** — "Meal plan in 30 seconds" vs "One-cook base week plan"
   - Metric: click-through to register
   - Variant: 5000 per variant, 14-day run
2. **CTA position test** — CTA for 2 times above the fold vs 1 time plus mid-page
   - Metric: conversion rate
3. **Social proof inclusion test** — CTA near testimonial vs separate testimonial section
   - .confidence review: 80%, to be reviewed
4. **Price display test** — "From $19 / Month Savings" vs "Two week free, then $19"
