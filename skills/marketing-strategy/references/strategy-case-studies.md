# Strategy Case Studies

One full case study: the solo-founder launch of "Chronofile" (a fictional daily journaling app) from concept to measurable strategy. This follows the sequence that the skill Workflow prescribes — STP → Positioning → Audience → Messaging → Channel → Campaign → Metrics. Because the product is imaginary, no real data exists, but the structure and decisions reflect realistic indie product outcomes.

## Case study: Chronofile ($8/month journaling app, solo founder)

### Segmentation and targeting

The founder identified four potential segments through competitor analysis and personal network:

| Segment | Pain | Size (est.) | Channel access | 
|---------|------|-------------|----------------|
| New-parenthood memory loggers | Overwhelmed, want simple spot | 200k potential | Reddit r/parenting, mom podcasts |
| Journal-exist for therapy | Mental health | 500k | Job-related subreddits, LinkedIn |
| "Second brain" note-takers | Power Evernote-users vs. product-builder fatigue | 1M+ (but crowded) | r/productivity, Hacker News |
| Digital minimalists | Prefer unnetworked tools that just work (one-time payment) | Smaller, but intense loyalty | r/minimalism, indie blogs |

**Chosen: Daily-paren journal loggers** because their need is highest (baby arrival), their purchase intent highest (they've already spent on supplies), and the channel more accessible to a first-time founder.

### Positioning statement

> "For new parents who want to remember every tiny moment without spending 30 minutes writing, Chronofile is the 90-second diary app that asks one question per prompt — unlike Day One or Penzu, we auto-fill context (weather, time, 3 tags) so the "real" writing is a single sentence."

**Rejected positioning:** "The simple journal that respects your time." (Too generic; any app can claim to be simple.)

**Rejected reasoning:** Without "target audience since new parents" as the audience, the product gets collapsed into "yet another diary app."

### 3. Audience research

Phone-chat conversations with 8 new parents (friends + from local parenting group):

- Functional Job: Log the day's moments.
- Emotional Job: Feel like a good parent because memory is preserved and shared.
- The key quote: *“I have a folder for my baby's first year photos; writing will never actually happen. I want a nudge.”*

Discovered: They need the habit built for them. The app must request the input at the time: end of night. which is just when they are exhausted. An evening notification will not work; they need a nag at 9PM with a single small visual prompt.

### 4. Messaging (primary: 3 tiers)

**Level 1:** "One minute before the moment passes."

**Level 2A:** "One question per day — answer in one sentence so you hit everything." (Cargo)

**Level 2B:** "Not a notebook. surveys the automatic context for time + location + availability and only asks you for the story part."

### 5. Channel strategy (scorecard)

| Channel | Fit | Effort (1-5) | Cost | Noise | Lifespan | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parens-blog guest posts | 5 | 3 | low | 3 | 4 | 15 |
| Reddit: multireddit | 4 | 2 (hard to gain trust) | 0 | 4 | 2 | 12 |
| Day podcast (next to start? | 5 | 1 | 0–300(single) at small | 3 | 3 | 12 |
| Instagram (parent aesthetic) | 2 | 3 | low | 5 | 2 | 12 |
| App store | 4 | 1 | 0 | 5 (quality) | 5 | 15 |

The debut was to get 2 guest posts on medium-sized parent blogs (top 10 Medium™), test 3 small Facebook groups ads (\$100 total for reach), and display a waitlist landing page.

### 6. Campaign brief

**Title:** "Chronofile launch: The 1-minute diary."

**Budget:** \$750 across 2 months.

| Week | Activity | 
|------|----------|
| 1–2 | Publish "The First Year Memory Blues" guest week 3 pitch |
| 3 | Launched a field in 2 parenting Facebook groups ($100) |
| 4 | Product Hunt launch (targeted solo-journal account — upload on Parenting tag) |
| 5 | Retarget with email follow-up for list / $150 more Insta ($75)
| 6 | Analyze CAC, double down on best organic channel |

### 7. Results (simulated for illustration)

- **Email list size:** 350 before launch
- **Launch day sales:** 22 ($16/month, 8:1) = 22 x $16/m = $352/m MRR day 1
- **CAC:** \$12.50 total (M of $750 / 60 total signups month)
- **Best channel:** Reddit direct (long term) — with zero spend — type of 50% organic lifetime

### Lessons

1. **The product template didn't change.** One unique designed app, but the niche audience defined the marketing.
2. **No media.** Guest posts were the primary source of high-converting leads. The step was following default.
3. **Hard to find high-int user:** The first week's list was built from parents; the second week's list from parents; the third: from parents. The best customers were also the first.

*Note: This case is synthesized from multiple successful indie daily app launches. Real working projects would differ per date, pricing, and distribution.*
