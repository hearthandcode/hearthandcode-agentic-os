# Business Plan Template

Full scaffold for the standard planning output. Instructional comments (in blockquotes) explain what belongs in each section; delete them before finalizing. Use with the planning workflow; fill every table. Pair with the one-page plan for stakeholder communication.

> **How to use this template.** Fill sections in order 1→10; sections 1–3 are commitments that constrain the rest, so do not start with financials. Replace every bracketed placeholder. Keep the finished document at 6–12 pages; longer means sections 1–3 are not yet decided, not that the business is complicated.

## Completion checklist

- Every bracketed placeholder replaced; no instructional blockquotes remain.
- Unit economics stated as three numbers (price, direct cost, contribution).
- Break-even computed founder-level, not overhead-only.
- Cash timing mapped: deposits, payment terms, trough month identified.
- Every high-impact assumption has a test with a falsification condition.
- Kill risks carry present/tested/owned mitigations with triggers.
- Commitment rules (spending, quitting, concentration caps) written with triggers.
- The document cites where each number came from; no invented precision.
