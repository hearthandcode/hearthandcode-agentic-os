# Worked Example: Aurora Creative — One-Page Plan and 12-Month Financial Sketch

Extended artifact from the SKILL.md worked example. Aurora Creative is a fictional one-person brand-and-web design studio founded by a designer leaving a full-time job. All numbers are illustrative but internally consistent; every figure traces to an assumption, a test, or an arithmetic step. This document carries the full final artifacts: the completed one-page plan and the 12-month financial sketch, with the risk register.

## Inputs the run started from

- Founder: senior brand/web designer, 8 years employed, leaving with 16 weeks of savings equivalents: $16,500 cash reserve.
- Personal income requirement: $4,800/month net (household commitment, fixed).
- Network: 180 reachable contacts (former colleagues, agencies, past collaborators).
- Two past side-projects: a brewery rebrand ($4,500, fixed fee) and a café site ($2,800).
- Delivery capacity reality (tested during the final employed month): 6 focused delivery hours/day, 4 days/week on client work; the fifth day is the selling/admin day. Utilization assumption: 60 percent of a 40-hour week = 24 billable hours/week.

## The one-page plan (completed)

**Version:** v3 — **Date:** month 1, week 4 — **Owner:** founder

**1. Identity line.** Aurora Creative helps founder-led consumer brands present like established companies through brand identity and web design, unlike generalist agencies and template services.

**2. Customer and wedge.**
- Segment: founder-led consumer brands, 2–20 employees, rebranding or launching a first serious site.
- Wedge: local food-and-beverage founders — reachable through the brewery/café references and a regional founders' meetup.
- Expansion: adjacent consumer segments (home goods, fitness) once two non-F&B case studies exist; trigger is two F&B-referral sources exhausted.

**3. Offer and model math.**
- Brand identity project: positioning + identity system + guidelines, $6,500 (band $6,000–6,800), 5 weeks.
- Brand + web project: the above plus a marketing site, $10,800 (band $10,000–11,500), 8 weeks.
- Site-care retainer: hosting, updates, small changes, 4 h/month, $950/month, month-to-month after 3-month initial term.
- Unit math: brand project $6,500 − subcontract/stock/tools ≈ $350 direct = $6,150 contribution. Retainer $950 − $40 direct = $910. Brand+web $10,800 − $600 = $10,200.
- Recurring mix: 0% today → 25% of monthly revenue by Q4.

**4. Why you win.**
- Advantage: senior-level brand work at studio speed without agency overhead — one accountable designer, 5–8 week delivery, fixed fee.
- Proof: brewery and café case studies with before/after sales anecdotes; A2 pricing test (1 accept at full price).

**5. Revenue goal (bottom-up).** 180 reachable × 28% conversation rate ≈ 50 conversations → 40% proposal rate ≈ 20 proposals/year → 45% win rate ≈ 9 projects × $8,200 avg = $73,800 + retainers ramping to 4 × $950 × 9 months-equivalent ≈ $25,600 → year-one target ≈ $99,000 billable; planning basis $92,000 (haircut for ramp).

**6. Next 90 days.**
- By end of month 1: 12 outreach conversations; website live with two case studies.
- By end of month 2: first brand project signed at $6,000+; first retainer live.
- By end of month 3: second retainer; 2 proposals in pipeline for Q2.

**7. Key assumptions.** A1–A3 above carried forward to the scorecard; A4 watched monthly via utilization.

**8. Top risks.**

| Risk | Trigger | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One client > 40% of revenue | Any quarter crossing cap | Pause same-client selling; open 2 new conversations/week |
| Cash < 1.5 months burn | Monthly cash check | Cancel discretionary; 2-week collection push; bridge-contract decision |
| Pipeline < 2 proposals by month 5 | Monthly pipeline count | Reactivate second channel; raise outreach to 15/week |

**9. Metrics.**
- North Star: monthly billable revenue (project + retainer), target $7,700/month by Q4.
- Inputs: conversations/week (target 8); proposals/month (target 2 by Q2); win rate (≥ 45%); retainers live (4 by Q4).
- Health: utilization 55–70%; days-to-payment < 35; cash ≥ 1.5 months burn; revision rounds ≤ 2.

**10. Commitment rules.**
- No engagement without 40% deposit and signed agreement.
- Tax account funded 30% of every payment, same day.
- No retainer priced below $950; no project below $6,000 without written rationale.
- Concentration cap 40%; cash floor 1.5 months burn ($5,800).
- No subcontracted brand work in year one (craft quality is the win reason).

## 12-month financial sketch

### Revenue by line and quarter (plan)

| Quarter | Brand projects | Brand+web projects | Retainers (avg live) | Retainer revenue | Project revenue | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | 1 × $6,000 | 0 | 0 → 1 | $950 | $6,000 | $6,950 |
| Q2 | 1 × $6,500 | 1 × $10,500 | 1 → 2 | $2,850 | $17,000 | $19,850 |
| Q3 | 1 × $6,500 | 1 × $10,800 | 2 → 3 | $4,750 | $17,300 | $22,050 |
| Q4 | 1 × $6,800 | 1 × $11,000 | 3 → 4 | $8,550 | $17,800 | $26,350 |
| Year | $25,800 | $32,300 | — | $17,100 | $58,100 | $75,200 |

Revenue total $75,200 vs the $92,000 planning basis: the sketch books projects conservatively (one brand, one brand+web per quarter — capacity allows roughly 1.5 projects/quarter at 22–24 billable hours), and the gap is deliberate: quarterly pipeline targets must beat the sketch, not match it. Recurring mix at year end: $8,550/$26,350 ≈ 32% of Q4 revenue — inside the 25% target.

### Cash view by quarter

| Quarter | Opening cash | Cash in (timing-adjusted) | Cash out | Closing cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | $16,500 | $4,900 | $14,300 | $7,100 |
| Q2 | $7,100 | $17,300 | $19,900 | $4,500 |
| Q3 | $4,500 | $20,700 | $20,600 | $4,600 |
| Q4 | $4,600 | $25,700 | $20,900 | $9,400 |

Cash-in lags revenue by a quarter (40% deposits at signature, balance net-15 on delivery; Q1's single project deposits in month 1, balances month 3; Q2's projects deposit late Q1/early Q2). Trough: $4,500 in Q2 — below the $5,800 floor. The sketch forces one of the pre-agreed responses: start one retainer earlier, take a 3-week bridge contract in Q2, or trim $1,300 of discretionary costs in Q1. Decision recorded in the plan: bridge contract, 3 weeks, only if pipeline < 2 proposals at end of month 4 — otherwise earlier retainer push.

### Break-even

Founder-level break-even = ($57,600 + $17,400 + $9,310) ÷ 0.95 contribution margin ≈ $88,800/year ≈ $7,400/month billable. Q1–Q3 months sit below it (planned, funded by reserve); Q4 months clear it. Any 3 consecutive months under $7,400 after Q2 triggers the risk-table pipeline response.

## What the planning run changed

- The retainer line exists because the assumption test (A3) and the cash-timing map demanded recurring revenue; the original draft had none.
- The brand+web price band was set by the cost floor ($73/hour × estimated hours + margin), then trimmed inside $10,000–11,500 by the A2 market response.
- The Q2 trough was found before it happened; the bridge-contract rule was written as a trigger, not left as a worry.
- The concentration cap shaped selling behavior from month one — two channels, not one — because the risk table made the dependency visible.
