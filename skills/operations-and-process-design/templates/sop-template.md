## Template Instructions (Delete Before Using)

If you are using this as a blank of a process to author yours, then:

- Replace all fields bracketed with `[...]` with actual content.
- Remove this "Template Instructions" section before distributing.
- The SOP prefix in the header is `ONB` for onboarding, `DEL` for delivery, `FIN` for financial, `INV` for investigation.
- The steps should all be serial single statements. If step 4 has two sentences, remove the second sentence.
- Prefer "If yes, do X" and "If no, do Y" to "consider" or "think about."
- If a step decides who receives a ticket, that step becomes the source of truth.
- Read the SOP aloud. If you skip a word, the operator will not know.
